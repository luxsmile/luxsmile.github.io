Dental hygiene care that comes to you

LuxSmile's mobile dental hygiene service brings the full hygiene appointment to you. Leili H. Zarrabi, RDH, arrives with portable, professional-grade equipment and instruments sterilized to the same standards as a dental office, then sets up wherever is most convenient for you.

In Ontario, Registered Dental Hygienists can practise independently, so you can book directly without a referral. CDCP and most insurance plans are accepted, with direct billing.

Who mobile care is for

Seniors & limited mobility

For clients who find travel difficult, including those recovering from surgery or living with a disability.

Care facilities

Visits for residents of retirement homes, long-term care and assisted living, arranged with staff and families.

Busy families

Appointments for parents and children at home, one after another, without the drive or the waiting room.

Workplaces

Cleanings at your office for individuals or teams, scheduled around the working day.

Services available at a mobile visit

  • Routine teeth cleaning (scaling and polishing)
  • Comprehensive oral health assessment, including gum measurements and oral cancer screening
  • Scaling and root planing (deep cleaning) for signs of gum disease
  • Fluoride treatment and desensitizing treatment for sensitive teeth
  • Professional teeth whitening
  • Personalized oral hygiene coaching, including care for dentures, bridges and implants
  • Referrals to a dentist or specialist when needed

Infection prevention & sterilization

Every mobile visit follows the same infection prevention and control standards as a dental office. Leili follows the College of Dental Hygienists of Ontario (CDHO) infection prevention and control standards and Public Health Ontario guidelines.

  • Instruments cleaned and sterilized before every visit, then kept in sealed pouches until your appointment
  • Sterilization equipment routinely monitored, with records kept
  • Single-use disposable items wherever possible, used once and discarded
  • Fresh gloves, mask and protective eyewear for every client
  • Equipment and surfaces disinfected before and after each visit

Mobile dental hygiene questions

Can a dental hygienist come to my home in Toronto?

Yes. In Ontario, Registered Dental Hygienists can practise independently. LuxSmile brings portable, professional equipment to your home, office or care facility in Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area.

Is mobile dental hygiene covered by insurance or CDCP?

LuxSmile accepts most insurance plans and the Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP), with direct billing. Coverage depends on your plan, so we recommend confirming it before your visit. We are happy to help you check.

What do I need to prepare for a home visit?

Very little. Leili brings all the equipment, including her own treatment chair if you like, and instruments sterilized to the same standards as a dental office. If you prefer, your appointment can take place in your own chair or on your couch. A nearby power outlet and a little space for the equipment are all you need.
