Healthy habits start early

Children should see a dental professional as soon as their first teeth appear. Early visits help catch cavities before they become serious, protect the baby teeth that hold space for adult teeth, and help children feel at ease with dental care.

Services for children & teens

Dental sealants

A thin protective coating on the deep grooves of the back teeth, where brushing can't reach. No drilling or freezing. Often placed when the adult molars come in, around ages 6 and 12.

Fluoride treatment

A quick fluoride varnish painted on the teeth to strengthen enamel and help protect against cavities.

Cleanings

An unhurried cleaning to remove plaque and tartar, with explanations children can understand.

Brushing & habits coaching

Practical tips for brushing, flossing, snacks and drinks, and habits like thumb-sucking, for children and parents.

Children's dental hygiene questions

At what age should my child have their first dental visit?

Children should see a dental professional when their first teeth appear, or by their first birthday.

What are dental sealants?

Sealants are a thin protective coating placed on the deep grooves of the back teeth. They help keep food and bacteria out of grooves that are hard to clean with a toothbrush. Placing them involves no drilling or freezing.

Is fluoride treatment safe for children?

Professional fluoride varnish is a widely used preventive treatment for children. We use an amount suited to your child's age and discuss any questions with you first.

Are sealants and fluoride covered by insurance or CDCP?

Sealants and fluoride for children are commonly covered by dental insurance plans and the Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP), subject to your plan. LuxSmile offers direct billing. Not sure if you're eligible or how to apply? We're happy to help you apply.

Can you see my children at home?

Yes. Mobile visits let you book appointments for the whole family at home, across Toronto and the GTA, including Downtown and Midtown Toronto, North York, East York, Scarborough, Etobicoke, Thornhill and Vaughan.
