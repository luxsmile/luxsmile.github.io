Signs of gum disease

Gum disease (periodontal disease) often develops without pain. Book an assessment if you notice:

  • Gums that bleed when you brush or floss
  • Red, swollen or tender gums
  • Bad breath or a bad taste that doesn't go away
  • Gums pulling away from the teeth, or teeth that look longer
  • Teeth that feel loose or are shifting

Early gum inflammation (gingivitis) can often be reversed with a professional cleaning and good home care. More advanced gum disease (periodontitis) needs a deeper cleaning below the gumline.

What is scaling and root planing?

Scaling and root planing is a non-surgical deep cleaning. It removes hardened buildup and bacteria from below the gumline and smooths the root surfaces, which helps the gums heal. It is often done over more than one appointment, one area of the mouth at a time.

Deep cleaning questions

How do I know if I need a deep cleaning?

Only an assessment can tell. Gum measurements show whether there are deep pockets or buildup below the gumline. If a regular cleaning is enough, we will tell you.

Does a deep cleaning hurt?

Comfort is different for everyone. We go at your pace, take breaks when you need them, and discuss options to keep you comfortable before we begin.

Is scaling and root planing covered by insurance or CDCP?

Scaling is commonly covered by dental insurance plans and the Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP), subject to your plan's limits. LuxSmile offers direct billing and can help you check your coverage.

Can gum disease come back after treatment?

Yes, which is why regular maintenance cleanings and daily home care are important. We'll recommend a schedule that suits your gums.

Can a deep cleaning be done at home?

Yes. Scaling and root planing is also available as part of a mobile visit at your home, office or care facility in the Greater Toronto Area.
