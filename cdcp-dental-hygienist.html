Dental hygiene care with your CDCP coverage

The Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP) is a federal program that helps cover the cost of dental care for eligible Canadian residents. LuxSmile is an independent dental hygiene practice that accepts CDCP, and we bill the plan directly so you don't have to submit claims yourself.

You don't need to see a dentist first. In Ontario, you can book directly with a Registered Dental Hygienist.

Hygiene services you can use your CDCP for

  • Teeth cleaning (scaling and polishing)
  • Oral health assessment and gum measurements
  • Deep cleaning (scaling and root planing) for signs of gum disease
  • Fluoride treatment
  • Pit and fissure sealants for children and teens
  • Desensitizing treatment for sensitive teeth

Coverage depends on the CDCP rules, your plan limits and your family income. Some services may need approval from the plan first. We review your coverage with you before treatment, so there are no surprises.

Who may qualify for CDCP

In general, the CDCP is for Canadian residents who:

  • do not have access to private dental insurance
  • have an adjusted family net income under $90,000
  • have filed their tax return in Canada for the previous year

Depending on your family income, the plan may cover the full cost of covered services or ask you to pay part of it (a co-payment). The Government of Canada decides eligibility. You can check and apply at canada.ca/dental.

CDCP questions

Do dental hygienists accept CDCP?

Yes. Registered Dental Hygienists can take part in the Canadian Dental Care Plan. LuxSmile accepts CDCP and bills the plan directly.

Do I need a dentist's referral to use my CDCP with a hygienist?

No. In Ontario, you can book directly with a Registered Dental Hygienist, without a referral.

Will I have to pay anything?

It depends on your family income and the CDCP fee rules. Some members pay nothing for covered services, and some pay a co-payment. We go over any cost with you before treatment begins.

Can I use CDCP for a home visit?

Yes. CDCP members can book mobile dental hygiene visits at home, at work or in a care facility in the Greater Toronto Area. We'll confirm what your plan covers before your visit.

I'm not sure if I qualify. Can you help?

Yes. Call or text 416-994-9669 and we'll help you understand the next steps. Eligibility itself is decided by the Government of Canada.
