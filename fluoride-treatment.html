What is a fluoride treatment?

Fluoride is a mineral that helps strengthen tooth enamel, the hard outer layer of your teeth, and makes it more resistant to the acids that cause cavities. A professional fluoride treatment is stronger than the fluoride in toothpaste and is applied directly to the teeth in a few minutes.

It is usually done at the end of a teeth cleaning, when the tooth surfaces are clean, and it can also be booked as its own short visit.

Who may benefit from fluoride?

Fluoride is not only for children. We may recommend it if you have:

  • A history of cavities, or early weak spots in the enamel
  • Dry mouth, including dry mouth caused by medications
  • Gum recession with exposed root surfaces
  • Sensitive teeth
  • Braces, bridges or other areas that are hard to keep clean
  • Children and teens whose adult teeth are still coming in

Whether fluoride is right for you, and how often, is decided at your assessment based on your own risk of cavities. You are always free to decline it.

Fluoride treatment questions

Is fluoride treatment only for children?

No. Adults can benefit too, especially with a history of cavities, dry mouth, gum recession or sensitive teeth. We recommend it based on your own risk, not your age.

How long does a fluoride treatment take?

The application itself takes only a few minutes. It is usually done at the end of a cleaning appointment.

Can I eat and drink after fluoride?

This depends on the type of fluoride used. With a varnish you can usually eat soft foods soon after, and we may ask you to avoid hot drinks, hard or sticky foods and brushing for a few hours. We'll give you exact instructions at your visit.

How often should I have fluoride applied?

It depends on your risk of cavities. Some people have it at each cleaning visit, and others need it more or less often. We'll recommend a schedule at your assessment.

Is fluoride treatment covered by insurance or CDCP?

Fluoride is covered by many dental insurance plans and by the Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP), often with limits on how often. LuxSmile offers direct billing. Coverage depends on your plan, so we recommend checking it before your visit, and we are happy to help. Not sure if you're eligible or how to apply? We're happy to help you apply.

Can I have fluoride applied at home?

Yes. Fluoride can be applied during a mobile dental hygiene visit at your home, office or care facility in the Greater Toronto Area.
