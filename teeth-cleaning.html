What's included in your cleaning

A professional cleaning removes the plaque and tartar (hardened buildup) that brushing and flossing at home can't reach. Each LuxSmile cleaning appointment includes:

  • A review of your health history and any concerns you'd like to discuss
  • Gum measurements to check the health of your gums
  • Oral cancer screening of the cheeks, tongue and soft tissues
  • Scaling to remove plaque and tartar above and below the gumline
  • Polishing and flossing
  • Fluoride treatment when recommended
  • Personalized advice for brushing, flossing and the tools that suit your mouth
  • Referral to a dentist or specialist if anything needs their attention

How often should you have a cleaning?

Most people benefit from a professional cleaning every 6 months. If you have signs of gum disease, a higher risk of cavities, or conditions such as diabetes, you may be advised to come every 3 to 4 months. We'll recommend a schedule based on your own mouth, not a fixed rule.

Sensitive teeth?

Let us know if your teeth are sensitive to cold, hot or sweet foods, or if cleanings have been uncomfortable in the past. We can go at your pace, take breaks, and apply a desensitizing treatment to help calm sensitive areas.

Teeth cleaning questions

Do I need a dentist's referral to book a cleaning with a dental hygienist?

No. In Ontario, Registered Dental Hygienists can practise independently, so you can book a cleaning directly with LuxSmile.

Is a teeth cleaning covered by insurance or CDCP?

Professional cleanings are commonly covered by dental insurance plans and the Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP). LuxSmile offers direct billing. Coverage depends on your plan, so we recommend checking it before your visit, and we are happy to help.

How long does a cleaning appointment take?

A first visit with a full assessment and cleaning is usually longer than a regular cleaning. We'll let you know how long to plan for when you book.

Can I have my teeth cleaned at home?

Yes. LuxSmile offers mobile dental hygiene, with professional equipment brought to your home, office or care facility anywhere in the Greater Toronto Area.

What is the difference between a cleaning and a deep cleaning?

A regular cleaning removes plaque and tartar above and just below the gumline. A deep cleaning (scaling and root planing) goes further below the gumline to treat signs of gum disease. Which one you need is decided at your assessment.
