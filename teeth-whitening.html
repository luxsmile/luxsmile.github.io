Is whitening right for you?

Professional whitening, also called teeth bleaching, lightens the natural colour of tooth enamel. It is often used for staining from coffee, tea, red wine or natural darkening over time.

It may not be recommended, or may need to wait, if you:

  • are pregnant or breastfeeding
  • have untreated cavities, or gum disease that needs care first
  • have significant tooth sensitivity or gum recession
  • mainly want to change the colour of crowns, veneers or fillings, which do not whiten

If whitening isn't right for you, we'll tell you honestly at your assessment and talk through other options.

Teeth whitening questions

How much does teeth whitening cost in Toronto at LuxSmile?

Professional in-office whitening at LuxSmile is $299 (standard), $399 (enhanced) or $499 (maximum). The option that suits you is recommended at your assessment, and your price is confirmed before treatment begins.

How long does a whitening appointment take?

About one hour in total, made up of 3 to 4 short sessions of about 12 minutes each.

Is teeth whitening the same as teeth bleaching?

Yes. Teeth bleaching and teeth whitening are two names for the same type of treatment: lightening the natural colour of your teeth with a professional whitening gel.

Will whitening make my teeth sensitive?

Some people notice temporary sensitivity during or after whitening. Your history of sensitivity is reviewed at your assessment and helps decide which strength is recommended. Desensitizing treatment is available.

Can whitening be done at home?

Yes. Professional whitening is also available as part of a LuxSmile mobile visit at your home in Toronto and the GTA.

Does whitening work on crowns or fillings?

No. Whitening lightens natural tooth enamel only. Crowns, veneers and fillings keep their current colour, which is discussed at your assessment.
