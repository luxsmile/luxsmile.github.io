What is a tooth gem?

A tooth gem is a small decorative gem bonded to the outer enamel surface of a tooth, usually one of the upper front teeth. The tooth surface is cleaned and prepared, and the gem is attached with dental adhesive and hardened with a curing light.

No freezing is used, no drilling is done and no tooth structure is removed. A tooth gem is a cosmetic procedure. It is not a treatment for any dental condition.

What to expect at your appointment

1

Assessment

We check the tooth and gums first. A gem can only be placed on a healthy tooth with natural enamel.

2

Placement

The tooth surface is cleaned and prepared, the gem is positioned where you'd like it, and the adhesive is hardened with a curing light.

3

Aftercare

You leave with written aftercare instructions for the first days and for everyday brushing around the gem.

Good to know before you book

  • A gem is expected to last approximately six months to two years, and may come off sooner. How long it lasts depends on your bite, your habits and your home care.
  • A gem cannot be bonded to a filling, crown, veneer or other restoration.
  • A gem cannot be placed on a tooth with decay or active gum disease. Those conditions need to be treated first.
  • A gem may need to be removed before certain dental procedures, some medical imaging, orthodontic treatment or whitening.
  • Whitening does not lighten the enamel under a gem, so a difference in shade may show if the gem is later removed. If you are planning teeth whitening, it is usually done first.
  • You can ask to have your gem removed at any time.

Caring for your tooth gem

  • Brush twice a day with a soft brush, passing gently over the gem rather than scrubbing it.
  • Avoid pressure on the front teeth, such as opening packaging with your teeth or chewing pens.
  • If you use an electric toothbrush or a water flosser, use a lower setting at that tooth.
  • Contact us if the gum next to the gem becomes red or tender, or bleeds when you brush.
  • Please do not try to remove or re-attach a gem yourself, and never use household glue.

Removal

Removal is done in the office. The gem is lifted off and the remaining adhesive is polished away from the enamel surface. If a gem comes off on its own, the tooth underneath is undamaged, and any adhesive left behind can be polished off at your next visit.

Does getting a tooth gem hurt?

The gem is bonded to the outer surface of the tooth. No freezing is used, no drilling is done and no tooth structure is removed.

How long does a tooth gem last?

A gem is expected to last approximately six months to two years, and it may come off sooner. It depends on your bite, your habits and how you care for it.

Can a tooth gem be placed on any tooth?

No. A gem needs healthy natural enamel. It cannot be bonded to a filling, crown or veneer, or placed on a tooth with decay or active gum disease.

What happens if my gem falls off?

The tooth underneath is undamaged. Any adhesive left on the tooth can be polished off in the office. Please do not try to re-attach the gem yourself.

Can I whiten my teeth with a tooth gem?

Whitening does not lighten the enamel underneath a gem, so a difference in shade may show if the gem is later removed. If you would like both, whitening is usually done first.

Are tooth gems covered by insurance or CDCP?

No. A tooth gem is a cosmetic procedure and is not covered by dental insurance or the Canadian Dental Care Plan.
