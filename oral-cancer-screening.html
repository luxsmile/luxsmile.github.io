A routine part of every visit

An oral cancer screening is a simple check, in the same way we check your gums and teeth. At every cleaning and assessment, we look at and feel the tissues of your mouth and neck to notice anything that looks different from usual.

Most changes turn out to be harmless. Checking regularly means we get to know what is normal for you, so anything new is easier to spot and can be looked at early.

What we check

  • Lips and the inside of the cheeks
  • Tongue, including the sides and underneath
  • Floor and roof of the mouth
  • Gums and the back of the throat
  • The jaw, neck and glands, by feeling for anything unusual

We look for things such as a sore that has not healed, a white or red patch, or a lump or thickened area.

What to expect

1

Look

With a light and a small mirror, we look carefully at the inside of your mouth.

2

Feel

With clean gloved hands, we lightly feel the jaw, neck and the tissues of the mouth.

3

Talk

We tell you what we saw in plain words and answer any questions you have.

It takes only a few minutes, with no needles and no drilling.

If we notice something

A screening is a check, not a diagnosis. If something looks like it needs a closer look, we will explain what we saw and recommend a next step, such as seeing your dentist or doctor, and we can write a referral for you.

Between visits, it is a good idea to let us or your dentist or doctor know about any sore or patch in your mouth that has not healed within two weeks.

Oral cancer screening questions

Does an oral cancer screening hurt?

No. It is a visual check with light touch to feel the tissues of your mouth and neck. There are no needles and no drilling.

How long does it take?

A few minutes. It is part of your cleaning and assessment visit, so there is nothing extra to book.

How often should I be screened?

We check at every cleaning and assessment visit, which for most people is about every 6 months or as we recommend for you. Between visits, tell us about any sore or patch that has not healed within two weeks.

What if you notice something unusual?

Most changes in the mouth turn out to be harmless. If we see something that needs a closer look, we will explain what we saw in plain words and recommend the next step, such as seeing your dentist or doctor. A screening is a check, not a diagnosis.

Do I need to book a separate appointment?

No. Oral cancer screening is included in your teeth cleaning and assessment visit, at our private dental hygiene studio or at your home.

Is oral cancer screening only for people who smoke?

No. We check every client at every visit, whatever their age or habits.
